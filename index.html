🛡️ Our 5-layer trust system

No single check is perfect. Stacked together, faking becomes harder than actually donating.

📱
1 · Phone OTP at signup

Kills bulk fake accounts instantly. One verified number = one profile. Same approach as India's e-RaktKosh portal.

📄
2 · Donation proof upload

Real donors get certificates from blood banks & camps. Upload yours → earn the 🟠 badge. Partnerships with Red Cross / Rotary verify at scale.

🤝
3 · Proof through action

Complete one donation through Raktdaan, confirmed by the recipient or hospital → 🟢 Verified Donor. Impossible to fake without actually donating.

🧠
4 · Logic-based fraud flags

Whole-blood donors must wait 90 days between donations. Claim weekly donations? Auto-flagged. Suspicious accept patterns? Blocked.

🔒
5 · Contact privacy by design

Phone numbers stay hidden until a donor accepts your request. Removes the motive for harassment and cash-for-blood scams.
